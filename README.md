## 🦠 Overview

**Panama disease** is a devastating fungal infection that impacts banana plants, especially the **Cavendish variety**, which dominates global markets.  
The disease is caused by the soil-borne fungus *Fusarium oxysporum* f. sp. *cubense* (Foc), which infects the plant’s vascular system, blocking water transport and eventually killing the plant.  

Once established, the fungus can **persist in soil for decades** — even without banana plants present — making eradication nearly impossible.  

---

## 🔬 How It Spreads

- The fungus enters the plant through its **roots**, spreading upward through the **xylem**.  
- It produces **thick-walled spores** that remain dormant for years, waiting for a new host.  
- **Race 1** caused the first global banana crisis by wiping out *Gros Michel* bananas.  
- **Tropical Race 4 (TR4)** now threatens *Cavendish* bananas — the current global standard — and several other varieties.

**TR4 was first discovered in Taiwan in the 1990s** and has since spread across Asia, Australia, Africa, and Latin America. Once a plantation is infected, the only viable response is **quarantine or abandonment of the soil**.

---

## 💰 Economic and Environmental Impact

- Bananas are one of the **world’s most traded fruits** and a vital food source in many countries.  
- **Small farmers** are especially vulnerable to economic loss when plantations are abandoned.  
- As infected land becomes unusable, **deforestation** and **land-use change** increase as farmers seek new growing areas.  

---

## 🧭 Our Mission

Our goal is to create an **accessible, data-driven tracking platform** that improves global awareness and response to Panama disease.  

Through our **interactive mapping tool**, we make information about TR4 outbreaks **transparent and actionable**.  
By enabling users to **contribute real-time data**, our system helps farmers, researchers, and policymakers monitor the disease’s spread and **coordinate effective containment efforts** — protecting banana production worldwide.

## ⚙️ Features

### 🗺️ Interactive Outbreak Map
Visualizes geo-tagged reports of Panama disease outbreaks across affected regions.  
Users can click data points to view detailed information about each case — including location, date, and report metadata.

### 📸 Image Reporting
Allows users to upload images of suspected infections for analysis or verification.  
Future integration may use **AI image recognition** to detect early symptoms of TR4 from leaf and soil photos.

### 🧠 Early Detection & Warnings
Potential use of **machine learning** or **AI-assisted models** to:
- Identify signs of infection from uploaded images.  
- Predict spread based on weather and environmental factors.  
- Alert users when local conditions increase risk.

### 🌤️ Environmental Awareness
Integrates weather and soil data to notify users when humidity, temperature, or rainfall create conditions favorable for fungal spread.

### 🛰️ Data Transparency & Research Support
All outbreak data is visualized on the map for transparency and global coordination.  
Researchers can analyze patterns, identify new high-risk zones, and compare with historical spread data.

### 📱 Offline Access (PWA)
Developed as a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** — allowing users in remote areas to:
- Submit reports offline.  
- Access cached data when internet connection is unavailable.

### 📚 Farmer Education
Provides accessible educational materials about:
- Preventing soil contamination.  
- Safe disposal of infected plants.  
- Equipment sterilization and biosecurity practices.
